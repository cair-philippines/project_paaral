About the School

Bagumbayan National High School is a public junior high school located in Taguig City, Metro Manila, under the Division of Taguig-Pateros. It serves learners from Grades 7 to 10 and is one of the priority institutions in DepEd's ESC program for the 2026–2027 school year. The school maintains a strong academic record and participates in various DepEd programs aimed at improving learner outcomes across NCR.

Admission Requirements

Applicants must present a Grade 6 Report Card (SF9), a PSA birth certificate, a barangay certificate of residency, and two 2×2 ID photos. ESC applicants must additionally provide proof of ESC eligibility — 4Ps household ID for Category A, or an income certificate for Categories B, C, and D. All documents must be submitted to the school registrar on or before the deadline set by the DepEd Division Office.
