Income classification figures used in the eligibility checker come from the PIDS (Philippine Institute for Development Studies) table provided by the user on 2026-05-04.

| Classification | Monthly Income |
|---|---|
| Poor | Less than ₱10,957 |
| Low income (but not poor) | ₱9,520 – ₱21,194 |
| Lower middle class | ₱21,194 – ₱43,828 |
| Middle class | ₱43,828 – ₱76,669 |
| Upper middle income | ₱76,669 – ₱131,484 |
| High income (but not rich) | ₱131,484 – ₱219,140 |
| Rich | ₱219,140 and up |

ESC eligibility (per Article III, E-GASTPE 2026 guidelines) covers Poor through Middle class. Upper middle income and above are not eligible under Categories B, C, or D (Category A SEG criteria may still apply regardless of income).

Note: Poor and Low income ranges partially overlap (₱9,520–₱10,957 is a near-poor zone). The UI groups "above ₱76,669" as a single ineligible tier.
